Frequently Asked Questions

Where do we board the speedboat in Mumbai? ▼

All our speedboats and yachts depart directly from Jetty No. 5, located directly opposite the entrance of The Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, Apollo Bandar, Colaba.

How much luggage can we take to Mandwa / Alibaug? ▼

Standard speedboats accommodate 1 suitcase per passenger plus hand baggage. For extra luggage or golf bags, we recommend booking our 12-seater Gulfcraft 31 or 18-seater Sea-Rider 38.

How does the Razorpay advance payment work? ▼

A nominal advance deposit (starting at ₹2,000) is paid securely via Razorpay to confirm the boat and skipper schedule. The balance is settled upon boarding at the jetty.

Can I chat on WhatsApp before making a reservation? ▼

Yes! Our WhatsApp desk is open daily at +91 7506920614. Message us anytime for custom schedules, catering, or yacht decoration setups.
