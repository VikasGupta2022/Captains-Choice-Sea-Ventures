Frequently Asked Questions

Where do we board the speedboat in Mumbai? ▼

All our speedboats and yachts depart from Jetty No. 5, located directly opposite the entrance of The Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, Apollo Bandar, Colaba.

How much luggage can we take to Mandwa / Alibaug? ▼

Standard speedboats accommodate 1 suitcase per passenger plus hand baggage. For extra luggage or golf bags, we recommend booking our 12-seater Gulfcraft 31 or 18-seater Sea-Rider 38.

How does the Razorpay advance payment work? ▼

A nominal advance deposit (starting at ₹1,500 - ₹3,000) is paid securely via Razorpay to hold the boat and captain schedule. The balance is settled at the jetty upon boarding.

Can I chat on WhatsApp before making a reservation? ▼

Yes! Our WhatsApp desk is open daily at +91 7506920614. Click the WhatsApp button on any screen to ask custom timings, yacht decorations, or catering options.
