Where do we board the private speedboat in Mumbai? ▼

All our private charters depart strictly from Jetty No. 5 at Apollo Bandar, directly opposite the grand sea entrance of The Taj Mahal Palace hotel in Colaba. Our dock master welcomes your party upon arrival, coordinates your luggage, and escorts you directly to the boarding pontoon.

How much luggage can our group bring onboard? ▼

Our flagship Speedster 24 features a dedicated watertight aft luggage bay designed to hold up to 1 medium trolley suitcase per guest plus standard soft duffels or hand backpacks (suitable for 6–8 passengers). For oversized sports equipment, golf kits, or pet travel, please notify our desk beforehand on WhatsApp.

How long is the crossing from Gateway to Mandwa (Alibaug)? ▼

Powered by twin Yamaha 200HP 4-stroke outboards and cruising at 32–38 knots, the 14-kilometer crossing between Jetty No. 5 and Mandwa Jetty takes approximately 18 to 20 minutes in typical sea conditions—saving over 3 hours compared to road traffic via the Mumbai-Pune/Goa highway.

How does the Razorpay deposit and payment confirmation work? ▼

To lock in your requested time slot, boat, and skipper, a secure advance booking deposit of ₹2,000 is processed through Razorpay (UPI, Google Pay, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Cards). Instant digital booking passes and boarding coordinates are issued immediately, and the remaining charter balance is payable at the jetty upon boarding.

What safety equipment is provided onboard? ▼

Passenger safety is our highest maritime priority. Every passenger is fitted with an Indian Coast Guard and ISO-certified life jacket before departure. Our vessel carries Garmin GPS chartplotters, VHF marine radios, twin-engine redundancy, first-aid medical kits, and is helmed by seasoned local mariners with over 15 years navigating Mumbai Harbour.

Can we arrange custom timings, return pickups, or sundowners? ▼

Yes! Because our speedboat is 100% private charter, we depart on your custom schedule between 6:00 AM and 8:00 PM. We also arrange evening sunset cruises in Mumbai Harbour, Elephanta Island heritage day-trips, and late-afternoon return pickups from Mandwa back to Gateway.
